## Evidence

Creatine is one of the most studied and effective supplements available.

### Muscle & Performance
- **Meta-analysis**: Increases lean mass, strength, and power output
- **Mechanism**: Increases phosphocreatine stores → faster ATP regeneration
- **Effect size**: ~5-10% improvement in high-intensity performance

### Cognitive Function
- **RCTs**: Improves working memory and processing speed, especially under stress or sleep deprivation
- **Mechanism**: Brain is highly metabolically active; benefits from ATP support
- **Particularly relevant**: For sleep-deprived or stressed individuals

### Longevity Relevance
- Supports muscle mass maintenance (sarcopenia prevention)
- May support brain health with aging
- Safe for long-term use (decades of research)

## Safety & Side Effects

**Well-established safety**:
- No kidney damage in healthy individuals (myth debunked)
- No hair loss causation (DHT study was single, unreplicated)
- Safe for long-term daily use

**Common effects**:
- Initial water retention (1-3 lbs) - normal, intramuscular
- May cause GI discomfort if not dissolved properly

**Hydration**: Ensure adequate water intake
