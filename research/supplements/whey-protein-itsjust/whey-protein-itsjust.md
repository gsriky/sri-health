## Why Whey Protein Concentrate

### WPC vs WPI (Isolate)

| Feature | Concentrate (WPC) | Isolate (WPI) |
|---------|-------------------|---------------|
| Protein % | 70-80% | 90%+ |
| Fat/Carbs | Small amount | Minimal |
| Lactose | Some | Very low |
| Cost | Lower | Higher |
| Immunoglobulins | More retained | Some lost |
| Processing | Less | More |

WPC retains more of the beneficial immunoglobulins and growth factors due to less processing.

### Benefits

- **Muscle protein synthesis**: Complete amino acid profile with high leucine
- **Convenient protein source**: Easy to hit daily protein targets
- **Fast absorption**: Ideal post-workout or between meals
- **Unflavored versatility**: Can add to smoothies, oatmeal, etc.
