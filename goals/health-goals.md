## Priority 1: Cardiovascular Risk Reduction (CRITICAL)

The lipid and NMR profile shows high atherogenic burden across every major marker. This is the single biggest modifiable risk factor.

### Lipid Targets
- [ ] ApoB <60 mg/dL (baseline: 109 — 82% over target)
- [ ] LDL-P <700 nmol/L (baseline: 2143 — 206% over target)
- [ ] LDL-C <70 mg/dL (baseline: 154)
- [ ] Non-HDL Cholesterol <100 mg/dL (baseline: 176)
- [ ] Small LDL-P <467 nmol/L (baseline: 672)
- [ ] Triglycerides <70 mg/dL (baseline: 102)

### HDL / Reverse Cholesterol Transport
- [ ] Large HDL-P >7.2 umol/L (baseline: <3.0 — critically low)
- [ ] HDL Size >9.0 nm (baseline: 8.2)
- [ ] HDL-C >50 mg/dL (baseline: 47)
- [ ] LDL/HDL Ratio <2.28 (baseline: 3.3)

### Lp(a) Management
- [ ] Monitor Lp(a) trend (baseline: 76 nmol/L, borderline elevated)
- [ ] Discuss PCSK9 inhibitor with physician (can reduce Lp(a) ~20-30%)

### Endothelial Function
- [ ] ADMA <100 ng/mL (baseline: 102 — impairs nitric oxide production)

### Screening
- [ ] Coronary artery calcium (CAC) score — establish baseline given high-risk profile

### Action Plan
1. Discuss statin or PCSK9 inhibitor therapy with physician — lifestyle alone unlikely to close the ApoB/LDL-P gap at this magnitude
2. Dietary interventions: reduce saturated fat, increase soluble fiber, add plant sterols
3. Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA-dominant) for triglyceride reduction and HDL improvement
4. Regular aerobic exercise (zone 2) to improve HDL function and particle quality
5. Niacin or fibrates may be considered for HDL-P improvement (discuss with physician)
6. Retest full NMR lipid panel in 3 months to assess intervention response

Both fasting glucose and HbA1c confirm prediabetic status. TG/HDL ratio of 2.2 (target <1.5) adds further insulin resistance signal.

### Glucose Targets
- [ ] Fasting glucose <90 mg/dL (baseline: 103)
- [ ] HbA1c <5.3% (baseline: 5.8%)
- [ ] Fasting insulin <6 uIU/mL (not tested — request next panel)
- [ ] HOMA-IR <1.0 (not tested — requires fasting insulin)

### Related Metabolic Markers
- [ ] Uric acid <5.5 mg/dL (baseline: 6.5 — correlates with insulin resistance)
- [ ] Triglycerides <70 mg/dL (baseline: 102 — insulin resistance signal)
- [ ] TG/HDL ratio <1.5 (baseline: ~2.2)
- [ ] eGFR >90 mL/min (baseline: 89 — slightly below, monitor)

### Action Plan
1. Dietary changes: reduce refined carbohydrates, increase fiber, time-restricted eating
2. Zone 2 cardio (150+ min/week) — most effective intervention for insulin sensitivity
3. Strength training (3x/week) — increases glucose disposal via muscle mass
4. Consider continuous glucose monitor (CGM) for 2-4 weeks to identify glucose spikes
5. Consider berberine or metformin (discuss with physician) if lifestyle insufficient after 3 months
6. Retest fasting glucose, HbA1c, AND fasting insulin in 3 months
