# Goals Folder Rules

This folder contains health goals, progress tracking, and milestone documentation.

## Purpose
Define clear health objectives and track progress toward longevity and quality of life goals.

## Key Files

### health-goals.md
- Master list of health goals
- Organized by category
- Includes measurable targets

### Progress Tracking
- `quarterly-reviews/` - Periodic progress assessments
- `milestones.md` - Achieved milestones and dates

## Goal Structure

### SMART Goals
Each goal should be:
- **Specific**: Clear, well-defined target
- **Measurable**: Quantifiable biomarker or metric
- **Achievable**: Realistic given current state
- **Relevant**: Aligned with longevity/QoL objectives
- **Time-bound**: Has a target date or review period

### Goal Format
```markdown
## [Goal Name]

**Target**: [Specific measurable outcome]
**Baseline**: [Starting point with date]
**Current**: [Latest measurement with date]
**Deadline**: [Target date or ongoing]
**Status**: [Not Started | In Progress | Achieved | Paused]

### Action Plan
1. [Specific action]
2. [Specific action]

### Progress Log
- YYYY-MM-DD: [Update]

### Related
- Protocol: [Link to protocol]
- Biomarkers: [Relevant markers to track]
```

## Goal Categories

### Metabolic Health
- Glucose control targets
- Insulin sensitivity goals
- Body composition objectives

### Cardiovascular
- Lipid panel targets
- Blood pressure goals
- Inflammation reduction

### Physical Function
- Strength benchmarks
- VO2max targets
- Flexibility/mobility goals

### Longevity Markers
- Biological age targets
- Key biomarker optimization

### Condition Management
- Symptom reduction goals
- Treatment effectiveness targets

## Review Cadence

### Weekly
- Check habit compliance
- Note symptoms/energy

### Monthly
- Review protocol adherence
- Assess subjective wellbeing

### Quarterly
- Comprehensive biomarker review
- Goal progress assessment
- Protocol adjustments

### Annually
- Full health review
- Goal setting for next year
- Long-term trend analysis
