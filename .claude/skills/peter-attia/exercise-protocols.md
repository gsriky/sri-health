# Peter Attia Exercise Protocols

Detailed exercise programming based on Peter Attia's Medicine 3.0 framework.

## Core Philosophy

> "Exercise is by far the most potent longevity 'drug.' The data are unambiguous: Exercise not only delays actual death but also prevents both cognitive and physical decline better than any other intervention."

## The Four Pillars of Fitness

### Overview

| Pillar | Weekly Time | Sessions | Purpose |
|--------|-------------|----------|---------|
| **Stability** | 1 hour | Daily (5-10 min blocks) | Foundation, injury prevention |
| **Strength** | 3-4 hours | 3-4 x 45-60 min | Muscle preservation, function |
| **Aerobic (Zone 2)** | 3-4 hours | 4 x 45-60 min | Mitochondrial health |
| **Anaerobic (Zone 5)** | 30-60 min | 1-2 sessions | VO2max, cardiovascular reserve |

**Total: ~8-10 hours per week** (Attia's personal commitment)

---

## Pillar 1: Stability

### Purpose
- Foundation that enables safe execution of all other training
- Injury prevention
- Long-term joint health
- Often the most neglected pillar

### Implementation
- **5-10 minute blocks** before strength or cardio sessions
- Focus on:
  - Core activation
  - Hip mobility
  - Shoulder stability
  - Spinal control
  - Balance work

### Key Concepts
- Stability ≠ Flexibility
- Controlled movement through full range of motion
- DNS (Dynamic Neuromuscular Stabilization) principles
- Breathing mechanics

## Pillar 3: Zone 2 Training (Aerobic Efficiency)

### Definition
**Zone 2**: The highest metabolic output you can sustain while keeping blood lactate below 2 mmol/L.

### How to Identify Zone 2
| Method | Zone 2 Indicator |
|--------|------------------|
| **Lactate meter** | <2 mmol/L |
| **Talk test** | Can talk, but not comfortably |
| **RPE** | 6-7 out of 10 |
| **Heart rate** | ~60-70% max HR (variable) |
| **Power/Pace** | Sustainable for 45-60 min |

### Weekly Volume
- **Minimum**: 3 hours/week
- **Optimal**: 4+ hours/week
- **Attia's personal**: 4 x 45-60 min sessions

### Modalities
- Cycling (Attia's preference - low impact, power measurable)
- Walking uphill
- Rowing
- Swimming
- Jogging (if tolerated)
- Elliptical

### Benefits of Zone 2 Training

| Benefit | Mechanism |
|---------|-----------|
| **Mitochondrial biogenesis** | Creates new mitochondria |
| **Mitochondrial efficiency** | Improves existing mitochondrial function |
| **Fat oxidation** | Trains body to use fat as fuel |
| **Lactate clearance** | Improves ability to process lactate |
| **Metabolic flexibility** | Switch between fuel sources |
| **Aerobic base** | Foundation for higher intensity |
| **Type I fiber development** | Slow-twitch, fatigue-resistant |

### Common Mistakes
1. **Going too hard** - Most people train above Zone 2
2. **Not enough volume** - 30 min 2x/week is insufficient
3. **Inconsistency** - Benefits require regular practice
4. **Using HR alone** - Heart rate varies; RPE/lactate more reliable

### Sample Zone 2 Session
- 5 min warm-up (below Zone 2)
- 45 min Zone 2 steady state
- 5 min cool-down
- Frequency: 4x per week

## Pillar 4: Zone 5 Training (VO2max / Anaerobic)

### Why VO2max Matters

> "VO2max is the best predictor of lifespan." - Peter Attia

| VO2max Level | Mortality Risk Impact |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Low → Average | 2x reduction in mortality |
| Low → Top 25% | 3x reduction |
| Low → Top 2.3% | 5x reduction |

### Split Recommendation
- **80% Zone 2, 20% Zone 5** (Attia's prescription)
- Example: 4 hrs Zone 2 + 1 hr Zone 5 per week

### Optimal Interval Length
**3-8 minutes** (NOT traditional 30-second HIIT)

Longer intervals allow you to:
- Reach and sustain VO2max
- Accumulate meaningful time at high intensity
- Actually stress the aerobic system

### Protocol Options

#### 4x4 Protocol (Attia's Recommendation for Most Patients)
- 4 minutes at maximum sustainable effort
- 4 minutes recovery (easy pace)
- Repeat 4x
- Total: ~32 minutes including warm-up/cool-down

#### Norwegian 4x4
- Same as above, but:
- Work: 90-95% max HR
- Recovery: 60-70% max HR

#### Attia's Personal Approach
- 25-30 min of very hard effort weekly
- Intervals of 4-8 minutes
- Uses power output on bike (not HR)
- RPE-based intensity

### How to Gauge Intensity
| Method | Zone 5 Indicator |
|--------|------------------|
| **RPE** | 9-10/10 |
| **Talk test** | Cannot hold conversation |
| **HR** | >90% max HR |
| **Power** | Near max sustainable |
| **Sustainability** | Can barely complete interval |

### Sample VO2max Session
1. 10 min warm-up (progressive)
2. 4 min HARD (max sustainable)
3. 4 min EASY (recovery)
4. Repeat 3-4 times
5. 5 min cool-down
6. Total: ~40-45 min

### Frequency
- 1-2 sessions per week
- Can add to end of Zone 2 sessions
- Allow 48+ hours between high-intensity sessions

## The Centenarian Decathlon

### Concept
Define 10 physical tasks you want to perform at 100 years old, then train backwards with "backcasting."

### Example Tasks
1. Carry groceries up stairs
2. Get up from the floor without using hands
3. Pick up a grandchild
4. Hike on uneven terrain
5. Open a heavy door
6. Get in/out of a car unassisted
7. Walk a mile without stopping
8. Climb a flight of stairs
9. Carry luggage
10. Maintain balance on one foot for 30 seconds

### Training Implications
Each task requires specific capacities:
- **Strength**: Lifting, carrying
- **Stability**: Balance, fall prevention
- **Cardio capacity**: Sustained activity
- **Mobility**: Full range of motion
- **Power**: Getting up, quick movements

### Backcasting
If you want to lift 30 lbs overhead at 100, you might need to lift 50 lbs at 80, 70 lbs at 60, etc.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Exercise is non-negotiable** - Single most powerful longevity intervention
2. **Zone 2 is foundational** - 3-4 hours minimum per week
3. **VO2max is critical** - "Best predictor of lifespan"
4. **Strength preserves function** - Train for the Centenarian Decathlon
5. **Stability prevents injury** - Don't skip the boring stuff
6. **Consistency > Intensity** - Decades of training matter most
7. **Start where you are** - 90 min/week = 14% mortality reduction
