# Peter Attia Biomarker Targets (Medicine 3.0)

This reference provides Peter Attia's recommended biomarker targets compared to standard reference ranges.

## Cardiovascular Markers

| Biomarker | Standard Range | Attia Target | Priority | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|----------|-------|
| **ApoB** | <100 mg/dL | <60 (ceiling), <40 (optimal) | CRITICAL | Causal driver of ASCVD. Lower is always better. |
| **Lp(a)** | <30 mg/dL or <75 nmol/L | Same (genetic) | HIGH | Test once. If elevated, aggressively manage ApoB. |
| **LDL-C** | <100 mg/dL | <70 mg/dL | HIGH | ApoB is more predictive, but LDL still useful. |
| **HDL-C** | >40 (M), >50 (F) mg/dL | Context-dependent | MEDIUM | Less actionable than ApoB. |
| **Triglycerides** | <150 mg/dL | <100 mg/dL | HIGH | Marker of metabolic health. |
| **Non-HDL-C** | <130 mg/dL | <100 mg/dL | HIGH | Good proxy when ApoB unavailable. |
| **hs-CRP** | <3.0 mg/L | <1.0 mg/L | HIGH | Inflammation marker. |
| **Homocysteine** | <15 umol/L | <10 umol/L | MEDIUM | Cardiovascular and cognitive risk. |

## Metabolic Markers

| Biomarker | Standard Range | Attia Target | Priority | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|----------|-------|
| **Fasting Glucose** | 70-100 mg/dL | <95 mg/dL | HIGH | Tighter control for longevity. |
| **Fasting Insulin** | <25 mIU/L | <10 mIU/L (ideally <5) | CRITICAL | Early insulin resistance marker. |
| **HbA1c** | <5.7% | <5.4% | HIGH | Long-term glucose control. |
| **HOMA-IR** | <2.5 | <1.0 | HIGH | Calculated insulin resistance index. |
| **C-Peptide** | 0.5-2.0 ng/mL | Lower end | MEDIUM | Insulin production marker. |
| **Uric Acid** | 3.5-7.2 mg/dL (M) | <6.0 mg/dL | MEDIUM | Metabolic syndrome marker. |

### CGM Targets (Continuous Glucose Monitor)

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Average Glucose** | <100 mg/dL | Non-diabetic target |
| **Post-meal Peak** | <140 mg/dL | Should not exceed 160 mg/dL |
| **Standard Deviation** | Minimize | Glucose variability harmful |
| **Time in Range (70-140)** | >90% | Goal for metabolic health |

## Functional/Fitness Markers

| Marker | Assessment | Attia Target | Priority | Notes |
|--------|------------|--------------|----------|-------|
| **VO2max** | Cardiopulmonary test | Top 20% for age (minimum) | CRITICAL | "Best predictor of lifespan" |
| **Grip Strength** | Dynamometer | Above average for age | HIGH | Predictor of all-cause mortality |
| **Muscle Mass** | DEXA scan | Preserve/increase | HIGH | Sarcopenia prevention |
| **Body Fat %** | DEXA scan | <20% (M), <25% (F) | HIGH | But context matters |
| **Bone Density** | DEXA scan | T-score > -1.0 | HIGH | Osteoporosis prevention |

## Genetic/Advanced Testing

| Test | Purpose | Frequency | Notes |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|
| **APOE Status** | Alzheimer's risk | Once | APOE4 = higher risk |
| **Lp(a)** | Cardiovascular risk | Once | Genetic, doesn't change |
| **Full-body MRI** | Cancer screening | Annually | Early detection |
| **Coronary Calcium Score** | CVD risk stratification | Every 5 years | If considering statin |
| **OGTT** | Glucose/insulin response | Periodically | More revealing than fasting |

## Attia vs. Standard Approach Summary

| Philosophy | Standard Medicine | Attia's Medicine 3.0 |
|------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Goal** | Detect disease | Prevent disease |
| **Timing** | After symptoms | Decades before |
| **Targets** | Reference ranges | Optimal ranges |
| **ApoB approach** | 10-year risk calc | "As low as possible" |
| **Metabolic** | Treat when diabetic | Prevent insulin resistance |
| **Screening** | Standard guidelines | Aggressive early detection |

## Key Principles

1. **Earlier is better** - Address risk factors decades before disease
2. **Lower is better (for ApoB)** - No lower limit has shown harm
3. **Individual variation** - Targets may adjust based on genetics, history
4. **Trends matter** - Direction of change as important as absolute values
5. **Functional measures** - VO2max, strength as important as blood markers
