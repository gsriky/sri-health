# Blueprint Supplement Protocol

## Philosophy

Bryan Johnson's supplement approach is based on three principles:
1. **Evidence-tiered**: Prioritize well-researched compounds
2. **Biomarker-driven**: Adjust based on individual results
3. **Synergistic**: Compounds work together, not in isolation

## The Three Tiers

### Tier 1: Universal
Supplements with strong evidence that most people benefit from, regardless of individual biomarkers.

### Tier 2: Personalized
Supplements added based on specific biomarker deficiencies or targets.

### Tier 3: Experimental
Cutting-edge compounds with emerging evidence; higher risk/reward.

## Tier 1: Universal Supplements

### Extra Virgin Olive Oil (EVOO)
**The Most Important "Supplement"**

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Dose | 30mL+ daily (2 tablespoons) |
| Type | High polyphenol (>500mg/kg polyphenols) |
| Timing | With meals |
| Brand | Blueprint "Snake Oil" or high-quality alternative |

**Why it matters:**
- Polyphenols: Antioxidant, anti-inflammatory
- Oleic acid: Cardiovascular benefits
- Oleocanthal: Natural anti-inflammatory (similar to ibuprofen)
- Supports brain health, gut microbiome, skin

**Selection criteria:**
- Harvest date within 12 months
- Dark bottle (protects from light)
- Polyphenol content listed
- Third-party tested

### NAD+ Precursors (NMN / NR)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Compounds | NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide) or NR (Nicotinamide Riboside) |
| Protocol | Alternating 6x per week |
| Dose | Varies (typically 500-1000mg NMN or 300-500mg NR) |
| Timing | Morning |

**Why it matters:**
- NAD+ declines ~50% between ages 40-60
- Critical for cellular energy (ATP production)
- Activates sirtuins (longevity genes)
- DNA repair, mitochondrial function

**Bryan's approach:**
- Alternates between NMN and NR
- Targets NAD+ optimization as cornerstone of protocol
- Monitors NAD+ levels (optimal: 40-100 μM)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Form | Creatine monohydrate |
| Dose | 3-5g daily |
| Timing | Any time (with food or shake) |

**Why it matters:**
- Well-researched (100+ studies)
- Muscle strength and power

### Vitamin D3

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Dose | 2,000-5,000 IU daily (adjust by blood level) |
| Target level | 40-60 ng/mL |
| Timing | Morning, with fat-containing meal |
| Form | D3 (cholecalciferol), not D2 |

**Why it matters:**
- Immune function
- Bone health (calcium absorption)
- Mood regulation
- Muscle function
- Cancer risk reduction

**Personalization:**
- Test 25-OH Vitamin D levels
- Adjust dose to reach 40-60 ng/mL
- Some need higher doses (5,000-10,000 IU)

### Omega-3 Fatty Acids (EPA/DHA)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Dose | 2-3g combined EPA/DHA daily |
| Ratio | Higher EPA often preferred |
| Form | Triglyceride form (better absorption) |
| Timing | With meals (fat helps absorption) |

**Why it matters:**
- Anti-inflammatory
- Brain health (DHA is structural)
- Cardiovascular protection
- Cell membrane fluidity

**Target:**

- Test via blood spot test

### Collagen Peptides

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Dose | 20-30g daily |
| Type | Hydrolyzed collagen peptides |
| Timing | With Vitamin C (enhances synthesis) |

**Why it matters:**
- Skin elasticity and hydration
- Joint health
- Gut lining support

**Notes:**

## Tier 2: Personalized Supplements

Add based on individual biomarker results.

### Magnesium

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Forms | Glycinate (sleep), L-threonate (brain), malate (energy) |
| Dose | 200-400mg elemental |
| When to add | RBC Magnesium <5.5 mg/dL or symptoms |

**Why it matters:**
- Involved in 300+ enzyme reactions
- Sleep quality
- Muscle function
- Blood sugar regulation
- Blood pressure

---

### B Vitamins

| Vitamin | When to Add | Dose |
|---------|------------|------|
| B12 | Level <500 pg/mL | 1000-2000 mcg methylcobalamin |
| B6 | High homocysteine | 25-50mg P5P |
| Folate | High homocysteine | 400-800 mcg methylfolate |
| B1 (Thiamine) | General support | 50-100mg |
| B2 (Riboflavin) | Methylation support | 25-50mg |

**Homocysteine protocol:**
If homocysteine >7 μmol/L:
- B12 (methylcobalamin)
- Folate (methylfolate)
- B6 (P5P)
- Betaine (TMG)

---

### Zinc

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Dose | 15-30mg |
| Form | Picolinate or citrate |
| When to add | Level <80 μg/dL or immune optimization |

**Why it matters:**
- Immune function
- Wound healing
- Taste/smell

**Note:** Don't exceed 40mg/day long-term; balance with copper.

---

### Vitamin K2

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Form | MK-7 preferred (longer half-life) |
| Dose | 100-200 mcg |
| When to add | With D3 supplementation |

**Why it matters:**
- Directs calcium to bones (not arteries)
- Synergistic with Vitamin D3
- Cardiovascular protection

---

### CoQ10 / Ubiquinol

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Form | Ubiquinol (reduced, more bioavailable) |
| Dose | 100-300mg |
| When to add | Age 40+, statin use, low energy |

**Why it matters:**
- Mitochondrial energy production
- Antioxidant
- Heart health
- Statins deplete CoQ10

## Supplement Quality Considerations

### What to Look For
- Third-party testing (NSF, USP, Informed Sport)
- Transparent sourcing
- Bioavailable forms
- No unnecessary fillers
- Certificate of Analysis available

### Forms Matter
| Nutrient | Preferred Form | Avoid |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| B12 | Methylcobalamin | Cyanocobalamin |
| Folate | Methylfolate (5-MTHF) | Folic acid |
| Magnesium | Glycinate, malate, L-threonate | Oxide (poor absorption) |
| Vitamin D | D3 (cholecalciferol) | D2 (ergocalciferol) |
| CoQ10 | Ubiquinol | Ubiquinone (if over 40) |
| Zinc | Picolinate, citrate | Oxide |

## Testing to Guide Supplements

### Baseline Panel
- Vitamin D (25-OH)
- B12
- Folate
- Homocysteine
- RBC Magnesium
- Zinc
- Ferritin
- Omega-3 Index

### Quarterly Monitoring
- Adjust doses based on levels
- Track toward OCOR targets
- Avoid over-supplementation

### Signs of Over-Supplementation
- Zinc: Copper deficiency symptoms
- Vitamin D: Hypercalcemia (rare)
- Iron: GI issues, oxidative stress
- Fat-soluble vitamins: Accumulation risk

---

## Contraindications & Interactions

### Consult Physician Before Starting
- If on blood thinners (omega-3, vitamin E interactions)
- If on diabetes medications (supplements affecting glucose)
- If pregnant or nursing
- If on statins (CoQ10 becomes more important)
- If taking any prescription medications

### Common Interactions
| Supplement | Interacts With |
|------------|---------------|
| Vitamin K2 | Warfarin (blood thinner) |
| Omega-3 | Blood thinners |
| Magnesium | Certain antibiotics |
| Zinc | Copper (balance needed) |
| Iron | Many supplements (take separately) |
