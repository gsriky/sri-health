# Blueprint Protocol Reference - The Five Pillars

## Overview

Blueprint is built on five interconnected pillars, with sleep as the foundation. The protocol is designed for systematic optimization through measurement, intervention, and iteration.

### Core Philosophy
- **Algorithmic self-governance**: Let data make decisions, not impulse
- **Consistency over perfection**: Same routine every day builds habit
- **Measurement drives action**: What gets measured gets improved
- **Prevention over treatment**: Optimize before problems arise
- **Evidence-based**: Prefer RCT-supported interventions

## Pillar 1: Sleep (Foundation)

Sleep is the single most important pillar - it affects every other system. Poor sleep undermines all other interventions.

### Bryan's Sleep Schedule
- **Bedtime**: 8:30 PM
- **Wake time**: 5:00 AM (before alarm)
- **Duration target**: 8+ hours
- **Sleep quality**: Maximize deep and REM sleep

### Sleep Environment Optimization
1. **Light control**
   - Blackout curtains (complete darkness)
   - No electronic light sources
   - Blue light blocking glasses 2 hours before bed
   - Dim lights in evening

2. **Temperature**
   - Cool room: 65-68°F (18-20°C)
   - Temperature-controlled mattress (Eight Sleep or similar)
   - Cooling during first half of night
   - Gradual warming toward wake time

3. **Sound**
   - Soundproofing or white noise
   - Eliminate disruptions
   - Consider sleep tracking for snoring/apnea detection

4. **Air quality**
   - Air purifier
   - Optimal humidity (30-50%)

### Pre-Sleep Protocol
1. **Last meal**: 6+ hours before bed (time-restricted eating)
2. **No caffeine**: After early morning (before noon ideally)
3. **No alcohol**: Disrupts sleep architecture
4. **Wind-down routine**: Last hour with family, no screens
5. **Lower heart rate**: Relaxation before bed (single most impactful free intervention)

### Sleep Supplements
- **Melatonin**: 300 mcg (micro-dose, not mg)
- Note: Most people take too much melatonin (3-10mg); 300mcg is closer to physiological

### Key Metrics to Track
- Total sleep time
- Sleep efficiency
- Deep sleep %
- REM sleep %
- Heart rate variability overnight
- Respiratory rate
- Sleep onset latency

### Tools
- Oura Ring
- Eight Sleep Pod
- WHOOP
- Apple Watch

## Pillar 3: Exercise

### Daily Commitment
- **Duration**: 60-90 minutes
- **Time**: 5:30 AM
- **Frequency**: Every day, no exceptions
- **Philosophy**: Same workout daily for habit formation

### Workout Structure
| Muscle Group | Exercise % | Rationale |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| Legs | 70% | Largest muscle groups, metabolic impact |
| Chest | 20% | Upper body balance |
| Back | 10% | Posture, pulling movements |

### Heart Rate Zone Distribution (Weekly)
| Zone | Intensity | Duration | Purpose |
|------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Zone 1 | Light | 1 hour | Recovery, baseline |
| Zone 2-4 | Moderate | 4.5 hours | Aerobic base, fat oxidation |
| Zone 5 | Vigorous (HIIT) | 1.5 hours | VO2max, metabolic boost |

### HIIT Protocol
- **Frequency**: 3x weekly
- **Duration**: 10 minutes each
- **Structure**: 4 rounds on treadmill or bike
- **Warm-up**: 5-10 min stability + dynamic warm-up
- **Result**: 22% increase in VO2max documented in studies

### Key Exercises
**Compound movements emphasized:**
- Squats (bodyweight, dumbbell, goblet)
- Lunges
- Leg press
- Push-ups
- Single-arm dumbbell rows
- Assisted pull-ups
- Russian twists

### Movement Throughout Day
- **Every 30 minutes**: 5 min light activity
  - Walking
  - Bodyweight exercises
  - Stretching
  - Light household activity
  - Dancing

- **Post-meal**: 5-10 minute walk after each meal
  - Glucose management
  - Digestion

### Weekend Activity
- Hiking
- Pickleball
- Cycling
- Climbing
- Outdoor exploration

### Critical Principle: AVOID INJURY
> "Injuries can create serious limitations, create body imbalances and cause long term complications. It's worth being cautious even if it means not doing certain things."

Injury prevention is prioritized over pushing limits.

### Fitness Metrics

- Grip strength

## Pillar 4: Supplements

### Three-Tier Philosophy

Blueprint organizes supplements into three tiers based on evidence and personalization needs.

### Tier 1: Universal (Most People Benefit)

| Supplement | Dose | Timing | Purpose |
|------------|------|--------|---------|
| Extra Virgin Olive Oil | 30mL+ | With food | Most important; polyphenols, fats |
| Vitamin D3 | 2,000-5,000 IU | Morning | Immune, bone, mood (adjust by levels) |
| Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) | 2-3g | With food | Inflammation, brain, heart |
| Creatine | 3-5g | Any time | Muscle, brain, energy |
| Collagen Peptides | 20-30g | With Vit C | Skin, joints, gut |
| NAD+ Precursors (NMN/NR) | Varies | Morning | Cellular energy (alternating 6x/week) |

### Tier 2: Personalized (Based on Biomarkers)

Adjusted based on individual blood work:
- Address specific deficiencies
- Target out-of-range markers
- Optimize toward OCOR

Examples:
- Low B12 → B12 supplementation
- High homocysteine → B vitamins (B6, B12, folate)
- Low magnesium → Magnesium supplementation
- Suboptimal D → Higher D3 dosing

### Tier 3: Experimental (Cutting-Edge)

- Newer compounds with less evidence
- Higher risk/reward profile
- Requires careful monitoring
- Examples: certain peptides, newer longevity compounds

## Implementation Guidance

### Starting Blueprint (Beginner)

**Week 1-2: Sleep**
- Fix sleep schedule
- Optimize environment
- Track with wearable

**Week 3-4: Add Exercise**
- Establish daily habit
- Focus on consistency over intensity
- Start with 30-45 minutes

**Week 5-6: Nutrition**
- Eliminate processed foods
- Time-restrict eating
- Add key foods (olive oil, vegetables)

**Week 7-8: Supplements**
- Add Tier 1 basics
- Get baseline blood work

**Ongoing:**
- Test biomarkers quarterly
- Adjust based on results
- Iterate and optimize

### Cost-Effective Blueprint

Majority of benefits come from free/low-cost interventions:
1. **Sleep optimization** - Free
2. **Exercise habit** - Free
3. **Avoiding bad things** (alcohol, processed food) - Saves money
4. **Basic whole foods diet** - Moderate cost
5. **Basic supplements** (D3, omega-3) - ~$50/month

Full protocol: $1,000-1,500/month
Core benefits: Much less

### Key Success Factors
1. Consistency over perfection
2. Measure to motivate
3. Build habits, not willpower reliance
4. Start with sleep
5. Professional guidance for medical decisions
