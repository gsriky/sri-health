# Blueprint OCOR Biomarker Targets

## What is OCOR?
OCOR (Optimal Clinical Outcome Range) is Blueprint's methodology for setting biomarker targets based on:
- Best available scientific literature for longevity outcomes
- Levels typically seen in healthy 18-year-olds
- More aggressive than standard clinical reference ranges
- Designed to prevent disease, not just detect it
