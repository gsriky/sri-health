## About Andrew Huberman

Andrew David Huberman (born September 26, 1975) is an American neuroscientist, tenured professor of neurobiology and ophthalmology at Stanford University School of Medicine, and host of the Huberman Lab podcast (ranked #1 health podcast globally).

### Academic Credentials
- **B.A. Psychology** - UC Santa Barbara (1998)
- **M.A. Psychology** - UC Berkeley (2000)
- **Ph.D. Neuroscience** - UC Davis (2004)
- **Postdoctoral Research** - Stanford (under Ben Barres)
- **Awards**: Cogan Award (2017), McKnight Foundation Fellow, Pew Foundation Fellow

### Research Focus
- Vision regeneration and repair from blinding diseases (glaucoma)
- Neural mechanisms of stress and resilience
- Neural plasticity - the nervous system's ability to change and learn
- Non-pharmacological interventions for anxiety
- Brain development and visual perception

### Outlive Framework: Medicine 3.0

Peter Attia's "Medicine 3.0" represents a paradigm shift from reactive to proactive medicine:

| Medicine Era | Approach | Focus |
|--------------|----------|-------|
| Medicine 1.0 | Pre-scientific | Observation, guesswork |
| Medicine 2.0 | Modern medicine | Treat disease after diagnosis |
| **Medicine 3.0** | Proactive longevity | Prevent disease, optimize healthspan |

### The Four Horsemen of Chronic Disease

Attia identifies four primary causes of "slow death" that Medicine 3.0 must address:

1. **Cardiovascular Disease** - Heart attacks, strokes, atherosclerosis
2. **Cancer** - All types, emphasis on early detection
3. **Neurodegenerative Disease** - Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, dementia
4. **Metabolic Dysfunction** - Type 2 diabetes, insulin resistance, NAFLD

### Key Outlive Concepts

#### Lifespan vs Healthspan
- **Lifespan**: How long you live (chronological)
- **Healthspan**: How well you live (quality of years)
- Goal: Extend both, but especially healthspan

#### The Centenarian Decathlon
A framework for defining physical goals for your last decade of life:
- What physical tasks do you want to perform at 80, 90, 100?
- Work backwards to determine training needs today
- Examples: Carry groceries, play with grandchildren, climb stairs, get up from floor

#### The Marginal Decade
- The last ~10 years of life often involve significant decline
- Medicine 3.0 aims to compress morbidity (shorten the decline period)
- Delay onset of the Four Horsemen as long as possible
