# Andrew Huberman Supplements - Complete Reference

This document provides a comprehensive guide to supplements discussed and used by Andrew Huberman, including dosages, timing, mechanisms, and important caveats.

## Huberman's Supplement Philosophy

### Core Principles
1. **Behavioral foundations first**: Sleep, light exposure, exercise, nutrition before supplements
2. **Single-ingredient supplements**: Easier to adjust doses, identify effects
3. **Cycle when appropriate**: Not everything should be taken daily
4. **Get bloodwork**: Know your baseline before supplementing
5. **Quality matters**: Third-party tested, reputable brands

### Decision Framework
Before adding a supplement, ask:
1. Is there a behavioral intervention that addresses this?
2. Is there a dietary solution?
3. Do I have bloodwork showing a deficiency or suboptimal level?
4. Is the research quality high enough?
5. What's the risk/benefit ratio?

## Sleep Supplements

### Core Sleep Stack

#### Magnesium L-Threonate (Magtein)
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Dose** | 140mg elemental magnesium (typically 2g of Magtein) |
| **Timing** | 30-60 minutes before bed |
| **Mechanism** | Crosses blood-brain barrier; enhances GABA signaling; reduces cortical excitability |
| **Research** | Shown to improve sleep quality, reduce time to sleep, enhance memory |
| **Notes** | L-threonate form specifically for brain; other forms (glycinate, citrate) work differently |

#### Apigenin
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Dose** | 50mg |
| **Timing** | 30-60 minutes before bed |
| **Mechanism** | Binds to chloride channels similar to benzodiazepines (but gentler); reduces anxiety |
| **Source** | Found in chamomile; supplement form more concentrated |
| **Notes** | May affect estrogen (research unclear); some advise caution for those with hormone-sensitive conditions |

#### L-Theanine
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Dose** | 100-300mg |
| **Timing** | 30-60 minutes before bed |
| **Mechanism** | Increases GABA, glycine, dopamine; reduces excitatory glutamate |
| **Notes** | Some find it causes vivid dreams (can be a pro or con); start at 100mg |
| **Caution** | Huberman notes some people (including him at times) find it causes too-vivid dreams |

### Optional Sleep Supplements (Cycle)

| **Timing** | Before bed |
| **Use case** | Middle-of-night waking |

#### GABA
| Attribute | Details |

## Foundational Supplements

### Vitamin D3
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Dose** | 5000 IU |
| **Timing** | Morning, with fat-containing meal |
| **Mechanism** | Hormone precursor; immune function; gene expression |
| **Notes** | Get blood levels checked; aim for 40-60 ng/mL; most people deficient |

### Vitamin K2 (MK-7)
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Dose** | 100-200mcg |
| **Timing** | With vitamin D |
| **Mechanism** | Directs calcium to bones (not arteries); works synergistically with D3 |
| **Notes** | Important when supplementing D3; supports cardiovascular and bone health |

### Fish Oil / Omega-3s
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Dose** | 2g+ EPA daily |
| **Timing** | With meals |
| **Source** | Quality fish oil or algae-based for vegans |
| **Notes** | Huberman's most-emphasized supplement; affects mood, inflammation, brain health |

| **Timing** | With meals |
| **Mechanism** | Cardiovascular support; may offset TMAO from fish/eggs |

## Supplements Huberman Approaches with Caution

### NMN/NR (NAD+ Precursors)
- Discusses frequently but notes limited human data
- Some concern about cancer promotion in certain contexts
- Suggests getting baseline NAD+ tested if pursuing

### Melatonin
- Generally recommends against regular use
- Can disrupt natural production
- Exception: jet lag (short-term, low dose 0.5-1mg)

## Quality & Sourcing Notes

### Recommended Practices
1. Choose brands that do third-party testing (NSF, USP, Informed Sport)
2. Single-ingredient supplements for dose control
3. Store properly (some need refrigeration)
4. Check for allergens and fillers
5. Start with lower doses, increase gradually

### Red Flags
- Proprietary blends that hide doses
- Unrealistic claims
- Unusually cheap prices
- No third-party testing
- Too many ingredients combined

### Huberman's Noted Brands
He has mentioned various brands but often emphasizes principles over specific brands. When he partners with brands (like Momentous), he discloses the relationship.

## Important Disclaimers

1. **Not medical advice**: Consult healthcare provider before starting supplements
2. **Individual variation**: Effects vary based on genetics, health status, diet
3. **Interactions**: Supplements can interact with medications
4. **Quality varies**: Supplement industry poorly regulated
5. **Foundations first**: No supplement replaces sleep, diet, exercise
6. **Bloodwork**: Get tested before and after to assess effects
