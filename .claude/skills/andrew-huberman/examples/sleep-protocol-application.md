# Example: Applying Huberman Sleep Protocol

This example demonstrates how to apply Andrew Huberman's sleep optimization protocol for a health tracking timeline entry.

## Scenario
User reports poor sleep quality and wants to implement evidence-based improvements.

## Assessment

### Current Issues Identified
- Difficulty falling asleep
- Waking at 3-4 AM
- Low energy in afternoon

### Relevant Huberman Protocols

#### 1. Morning Light Exposure
**Protocol**: View natural sunlight within 30-60 minutes of waking
- Duration: 10 min (clear day) to 30 min (cloudy)
- No sunglasses
- Sets circadian rhythm for better nighttime sleep

**Implementation**:
```
Morning Routine Addition:
- 7:00 AM: Wake
- 7:15-7:30 AM: Outdoor walk or sit on porch facing east
- Track: Note whether sunny/cloudy, duration
```

#### 2. Caffeine Timing
**Protocol**: Delay caffeine 90-120 minutes after waking
- Prevents afternoon crash
- Allows natural adenosine clearing

**Implementation**:
```
Before: Coffee immediately upon waking (6:30 AM)
After: Coffee at 8:30-9:00 AM
Track: Energy levels at 2 PM, 4 PM, 6 PM
```

#### 3. Temperature Optimization
**Protocol**: Cool bedroom, warm extremities
- Bedroom: 65-68°F (18-20°C)
- Hot shower 1-2 hours before bed

**Implementation**:
```
- Set thermostat to 67°F at 9 PM
- 8:30 PM: Hot shower (10-15 min)
- Lightweight bedding, no heavy socks
```

#### 4. Evening Light Management
**Protocol**: Dim lights after sunset, avoid bright light 10 PM - 4 AM
- Use low, warm lighting
- Minimize screen brightness

**Implementation**:
```
- 8:00 PM: Dim overhead lights, switch to lamps
- 9:00 PM: Screen devices on lowest brightness + night shift
- No bright bathroom lights during night waking
```

#### 5. Sleep Supplement Stack
**Protocol**: 30-60 minutes before bed

| Supplement | Dose | Notes |
|------------|------|-------|
| Magnesium L-threonate | 140mg Mg | Core stack |
| Apigenin | 50mg | Core stack |
| L-theanine | 100mg | Start low |
| Myo-inositol | 900mg | For 3 AM waking (not nightly) |

2. **Consider interactions**
   - Check current supplement list for overlaps
   - Note any medications that affect sleep

3. **Track in biomarkers**
   - Create dated folder: `biomarkers/YYYY-MM-DD-huberman-sleep-protocol/`
   - Include daily tracking sheet
   - Note subjective and objective measures

4. **Compare with Blueprint**
   - Huberman's behavioral-first approach complements Blueprint
   - Both emphasize light exposure and consistent timing
   - Can use both frameworks for comprehensive optimization
