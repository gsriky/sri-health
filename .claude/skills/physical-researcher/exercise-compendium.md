## Calorie Calculation Quick Reference

**Formula**: Calories = MET x Weight(kg) x Duration(hours)

**Example** (80 kg person, 45 min running at 6 mph):
- MET = 9.8
- Calories = 9.8 x 80 x 0.75 = 588 kcal

**Corrected MET Formula** (more accurate for individuals):
- Corrected MET = Standard MET x (3.5 / individual resting VO2)
- Use when individual resting metabolic rate is known

## Notes on MET Accuracy

- MET values represent averages across populations
- Individual variation: +/- 10-20% is common
- Factors affecting accuracy: fitness level, body composition, age, sex, environmental conditions
- Higher-fit individuals may have lower relative cost for same absolute activity
- Heart rate monitors + VO2 provide more accurate individual estimates
- The 2024 Compendium introduced age-adjusted METs for older adults (60+)
