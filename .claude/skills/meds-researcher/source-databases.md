# Scientific Source Databases

## Primary Databases

| Database | URL | Best For | Access |
|----------|-----|----------|--------|
| **PubMed** | pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov | Clinical trials, meta-analyses, systematic reviews | Free (abstracts always; some full-text via PMC) |
| **Examine.com** | examine.com/supplements/ | Supplement evidence summaries, dosage ranges | Free summaries; full access paid |
| **Google Scholar** | scholar.google.com | Broad academic search, citation tracking | Free |
| **ScienceDirect** | sciencedirect.com | Full-text journal articles (Elsevier) | Abstracts free; full-text often paywalled |
| **Cochrane Library** | cochranelibrary.com | Gold-standard systematic reviews | Some free; most require access |
| **ClinicalTrials.gov** | clinicaltrials.gov | Ongoing/completed clinical trials | Free |
| **PMC (PubMed Central)** | ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc | Free full-text biomedical articles | Free |

## Interaction & Safety Databases

| Database | URL | Best For |
|----------|-----|----------|
| **Drugs.com** | drugs.com/drug_interactions.html | Drug-drug and drug-supplement interactions |
| **Natural Medicines** | naturalmedicines.therapeuticresearch.com | Professional-grade supplement monographs |
| **WebMD Interaction Checker** | webmd.com/interaction-checker | Quick consumer-level interaction check |
| **NIH ODS** | ods.od.nih.gov | Official supplement fact sheets |

## Product Quality Databases

| Database | URL | Best For |
|----------|-----|----------|
| **ConsumerLab** | consumerlab.com | Independent product testing, purity, label accuracy |
| **NSF International** | nsf.org | Supplement certification verification |
| **USP Verified** | usp.org/verification-services | Gold-standard supplement verification |
| **Labdoor** | labdoor.com | Supplement rankings by quality |

## Search Strategy by Research Type

### Supplement Research
```
PubMed: "[topic] supplementation systematic review"
PubMed: "[topic] randomized controlled trial"
Examine: https://examine.com/supplements/[topic]/
Google Scholar: "[topic] clinical trial efficacy safety dosage"
```

### Interaction Check
```
Drugs.com: interaction checker tool
PubMed: "[substance1] [substance2] interaction pharmacokinetic"
Google Scholar: "[substance1] [substance2] concurrent administration"
```

### Product Comparison
```
ConsumerLab: "[topic] supplement review"
WebSearch: "[topic] supplement third party testing comparison [current year]"
Amazon: best sellers in category
```

### Mechanism of Action
```
PubMed: "[topic] mechanism of action molecular pathway"
PMC: "[topic] pharmacology review"
```

## Access Notes

- **PubMed abstracts** are always accessible via WebFetch
- **PMC full-text** articles are freely accessible
- **Examine.com** free pages contain substantial evidence summaries
- **ScienceDirect** abstracts are free; full text often requires institutional access
- **Cochrane** plain language summaries are free
- When a source is paywalled, extract what's available from the abstract and note the limitation
