## Ingredient Analysis

### Sensoril® Ashwagandha (250mg per capsule / 500mg daily)

**What it is**: Patented ashwagandha extract standardized for withanolide glycoside conjugates. Uses both root and leaf (vs KSM-66 which is root-only).

**Evidence**:
- RCT (n=64): 300mg 2x/day reduced cortisol by 27.9% over 60 days
- RCT: Improved stress, anxiety, and serum cortisol vs placebo
- May support hormonal balance in men under stress

**Note**: Sensoril uses a different extraction than KSM-66. Both are well-studied; Sensoril may be slightly more calming, KSM-66 slightly more energizing.

---

### Rhodiola rosea (125mg per capsule / 250mg daily)

**What it is**: Adaptogenic herb standardized to 3% rosavins, 1% salidroside.

**Evidence**:
- Multiple RCTs show improved stress resilience and reduced fatigue
- May improve mental performance under stress
- Generally well-tolerated

**Timing consideration**: Some find rhodiola stimulating; morning dosing may be preferable if this occurs.

---

### Magnolia officinalis (100mg per capsule / 200mg daily)

**What it is**: Bark extract standardized to 2% honokiol.

**Evidence**:
- Honokiol is a GABA-A receptor modulator (similar mechanism to benzodiazepines but milder)
- Studies show anxiolytic effects
- May help reduce cortisol spikes
- Supports sleep when taken in evening

**Note**: This is likely the most directly calming ingredient in the formula.

---

### L-Theanine (50mg per capsule / 100mg daily)

**What it is**: Amino acid found in tea leaves.

**Evidence**:
- Promotes alpha brain waves (relaxed alertness)
- RCTs show reduced stress and anxiety
- Typical effective dose: 100-200mg
- No sedation or tolerance buildup

**Note**: 100mg daily is on the lower end; some may benefit from additional L-theanine supplementation (100-200mg more).

---

### Vitamin D3 (400 IU per capsule / 800 IU daily)

**What it is**: Cholecalciferol derived from lanolin.

**Evidence**:
- Essential for immune function, mood, hormones
- 800 IU is a maintenance dose, not therapeutic
- Most longevity protocols recommend 2,000-5,000 IU daily

**Note**: If taking Cortisol Calm, you're getting 800 IU D3. Account for this in total daily D3 intake.
