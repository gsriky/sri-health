### Mechanism of Action

Berberine activates **AMPK (adenosine monophosphate-activated protein kinase)**, a central regulator of cellular energy homeostasis. This produces:

1. **Insulin sensitization** -- Enhances glucose uptake in muscle cells independent of insulin
2. **Hepatic glucose output reduction** -- Decreases liver gluconeogenesis
3. **Lipid metabolism** -- Upregulates LDL receptors (increases LDL clearance), inhibits PCSK9 (similar mechanism to Repatha), reduces hepatic lipogenesis
4. **Gut microbiome** -- Modulates gut bacteria favorably, reduces endotoxin-driven inflammation
5. **PCSK9 inhibition** -- Berberine naturally inhibits PCSK9, the same target as Repatha (evolocumab), though at a much lower potency

## Evidence

### Lipid Effects (Meta-analyses)

- **LDL-C reduction**: 20-25% (comparable to low-dose statin)
- **Triglyceride reduction**: 25-35%
- **Total cholesterol reduction**: 15-20%
- **HDL increase**: Modest, 2-5%

Source: Frontiers in Pharmacology systematic review and meta-analysis (2025) -- 1.5g/day optimal for lipid effects

### Insulin Resistance Effects (Meta-analyses)

- **Fasting insulin reduction**: 28-45%
- **HOMA-IR reduction**: 28-45%
- **Fasting glucose reduction**: 10-15 mg/dL
- **HbA1c reduction**: 0.5-1.0% in diabetic patients, less in pre-diabetic

Source: Frontiers in Pharmacology (2025) -- 1.8g/day optimal for insulin/HOMA-IR

### Comparison to Statins

- Berberine reduces LDL-C similarly to low-dose statins (10-20mg simvastatin equivalent)
- Berberine has additional metabolic benefits statins don't provide (insulin sensitization, glucose reduction)
- Can be combined with statins for additive effect
- Does NOT have statin side effects (myalgia, liver enzyme elevation)

## Side Effects

- **GI effects** (most common): Diarrhea, constipation, gas, abdominal pain -- usually mild and transient
- **Mitigation**: Take with meals, start at lower dose (500mg 2x/day), increase gradually
- **Rare**: Headache, skin rash
- **Long-term safety**: Generally well-tolerated in studies up to 1 year

## Monitoring

- **3-month retest**: Fasting insulin, glucose, HOMA-IR, lipid panel with Cardio IQ
- **Watch for**: GI side effects (first 1-2 weeks), any unusual symptoms
- **Track**: Blood sugar if available (CGM would be ideal)
- **Liver enzymes**: ALT/AST at next blood draw (currently 36/22 -- ALT slightly elevated by OCOR)
