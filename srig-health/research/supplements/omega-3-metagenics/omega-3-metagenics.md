## Rationale

### Baseline Values (2026-01-23)

| Biomarker | Value | Optimal |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| OmegaCheck (EPA+DPA+DHA) | 5.3% | >=5.5% |
| Omega-6/Omega-3 Ratio | 6.3 | <4:1 |
| Omega-3 Index (EPA+DHA) | ~4.0% | >8% |

### Target

- Increase OmegaCheck to >8%
- Reduce Omega-6/Omega-3 ratio to <4:1
- Support cardiovascular, brain, and joint health

## Evidence

- Omega-3 supplementation improves cardiovascular outcomes (REDUCE-IT trial)
- EPA+DHA at 1-2g daily effective for raising omega-3 index
- Triglyceride form has ~70% better absorption than ethyl ester form
- High EPA:DHA ratio (2.4:1) beneficial for inflammation

## Monitoring

- **Retest**: OmegaCheck at next blood panel (~3 months)
- **Track**: hs-CRP for inflammation correlation
- **Watch for**: Fish burps, GI upset (unlikely with this formulation)

## Notes

- Taking with lunch as it's typically a fattier meal
- May increase to 2 softgels (2000mg) if levels don't improve after 3 months
