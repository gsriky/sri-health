## Dosing

- **Dose**: 1 capsule (50 billion CFU)
- **Timing**: 30 minutes before breakfast
- **With/Without Food**: Empty stomach

### Why 30 Minutes Before Breakfast?

1. **Lowest stomach acid** - After overnight fast, stomach pH is highest (least acidic), maximizing bacterial survival
2. **Fastest transit** - Empty stomach allows quick passage to intestines where colonization occurs
3. **Research-backed** - Studies show best probiotic survival when taken 30 min before meal or with meal, worst after meal
4. **Delayed-release backup** - Even though timing is optimized, the capsule technology provides additional acid protection

## Rationale

### Chronic Urticaria Connection

Research shows chronic urticaria patients have significantly reduced levels of both Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium compared to healthy controls. This multi-strain product addresses both deficiencies.

### Key Evidence

- Meta-analysis: Probiotics + antihistamines show OR 2.90 improvement over antihistamines alone for urticaria
- L. salivarius LS01 + B. breve BR03 specifically studied for chronic spontaneous urticaria
- 26% of patients showed improvement in urticaria clinical trial

### Secondary Benefits

- L. gasseri & B. lactis may support glucose optimization (fasting glucose at 99 mg/dL)
- Broad strain diversity supports overall gut microbiome health
- May indirectly support hormone metabolism
