## Daily Schedule

**Morning (30 min before breakfast):**
- Probiotic 1 cap (50B CFU, 11 strains) — empty stomach optimal for bacterial survival

**Morning (with breakfast):**
- Vitamin D — fat-soluble, absorbs ~32% better with fat (eggs, etc.)
- Berberine 500mg (1 cap) — take with meals to reduce GI side effects

**Morning (sipped throughout):**
- Creatine 5g — dissolved in water, sipped over a few hours

**Lunch (with meal):**
- Omega-3 1 softgel (1000mg EPA+DHA)

**Afternoon (~3pm):**
- Whey Protein 1 scoop (~25g protein)

**Dinner (with meal):**
- Berberine 500mg (1 cap) — second dose with dinner
- Cortisol Calm 1 cap
