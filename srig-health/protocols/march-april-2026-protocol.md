## 1. NUTRITION PLAN

### Philosophy
Combines Attia's three-lever framework with Blueprint's plant-forward approach, adapted entirely for **Indian vegetarian cuisine**. Indian food is naturally advantageous: rich in lentils/legumes (soluble fiber, plant protein), turmeric (anti-inflammatory), fenugreek (glucose-lowering), and fermented foods (dosa batter, idli, dahi). Primary goals: reverse insulin resistance, reduce atherogenic lipoproteins, AND reverse hepatic steatosis.

### Vegetarian Protein Strategy
Getting 150-170g protein/day as vegetarian requires intentional planning. Key sources:

| Source | Protein per serving | Notes |
|--------|-------------------|-------|
| Paneer (100g) | 18-20g | Limit to 3-4x/week — higher in saturated fat |

## 7. RISKS & CONTRAINDICATIONS

### Vegetarian-Specific Nutritional Risks
- **Protein deficiency**: Must actively track protein intake, especially in first 2 weeks. Whey protein supplementation is likely necessary to consistently hit 150-170g/day
- **B12 deficiency**: Non-negotiable supplementation at 1000 mcg/day. Monitor levels every 6 months
- **Omega-3 insufficiency**: Without fish, algae-based supplementation is essential. ALA from walnuts/flaxseed converts poorly (5-10%) to EPA/DHA
- **Creatine stores**: Vegetarians have 20-30% lower baseline creatine stores. 5g daily supplementation is even more important
- **Iron**: Paradoxically, Sri has ELEVATED ferritin (120), so iron deficiency is NOT a concern despite vegetarian diet. Drink tea with meals to reduce absorption
- **Zinc**: Phytates in dal/legumes can impair zinc absorption. Monitor if energy/immunity decline. Consider adding soaked/sprouted legumes to reduce phytate content

### Supplement Interactions to Monitor
- **Berberine + any future statin**: Both are hepatically metabolized. Monitor liver enzymes (ALT, AST). Berberine inhibits CYP3A4 — can increase statin levels. If statin prescribed, discuss berberine dose adjustment with physician. **Extra caution warranted given hepatic steatosis — liver is already under metabolic stress**
- **Berberine + metformin**: Additive glucose-lowering — risk of hypoglycemia. Don't combine without physician guidance
- **High-dose omega-3 (>3g)**: Mild increased bleeding risk. Inform physician/dentist before any procedures
- **Magnesium (total ~544mg elemental/day)**: Can cause loose stools. Split dosing mitigates this
- **L-citrulline + blood pressure meds**: Additive hypotensive effect if on antihypertensives
- **NAC**: Generally well-tolerated. Can cause nausea/GI upset. Rare: may lower blood pressure slightly. Do not combine with nitroglycerin

### Warning Signs — Contact Physician If:
- Fasting glucose >115 mg/dL or symptoms of hyperglycemia
- Muscle pain/weakness (if statin started — rhabdomyolysis screening)
- Persistent GI distress from berberine >2 weeks
- Heart palpitations, chest pain, or unusual shortness of breath during exercise
- Signs of hypoglycemia (shakiness, sweating, confusion) — especially if berberine + exercise
- Yellowing skin/eyes (hepatic concern — **especially important given steatosis**)
- Right upper quadrant pain or tenderness (liver)
- Unexplained fatigue or nausea (could indicate liver enzyme elevation)
