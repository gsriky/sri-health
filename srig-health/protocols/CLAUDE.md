# Protocols Folder Rules

This folder contains health intervention protocols, treatment plans, and lifestyle programs.

## Purpose
Document and track evidence-based health interventions being followed or considered.

## Protocol Structure

Every protocol document should include:

### 1. Overview
- **Name**: Protocol identifier
- **Purpose**: What condition/goal it addresses
- **Status**: Active | Paused | Considering | Discontinued
- **Start Date**: When initiated
- **Review Date**: When to reassess

### 2. Evidence Base
- Supporting research (link to research/ folder)
- Mechanism of action
- Expected outcomes
- Evidence quality rating

### 3. Implementation
- Specific steps and dosages
- Timing and frequency
- Required supplies/medications
- Cost considerations

### 4. Monitoring
- Biomarkers to track
- Symptoms to monitor
- Frequency of assessment
- Success criteria

### 5. Risks & Contraindications
- Known side effects
- Drug interactions
- Contraindications
- Warning signs to watch for

### 6. Progress Log
- Dated entries on observations
- Adjustments made
- Results noted

## File Organization
- `README.md` - Overview and index of protocols
- `active/` - Currently followed protocols
- `considering/` - Protocols under research
- `discontinued/` - Past protocols with outcomes

## Protocol Categories

### Supplementation
- Foundational (vitamins, minerals)
- Longevity-focused (NMN, resveratrol, etc.)
- Condition-specific

### Lifestyle
- Sleep optimization
- Exercise programs
- Nutrition/diet plans
- Stress management
- Fasting protocols

### Medical
- Prescription medications
- Procedures
- Therapies

## When Modifying Protocols
- Document reason for change
- Note date of modification
- Track outcomes after changes
- Consider consulting healthcare provider
