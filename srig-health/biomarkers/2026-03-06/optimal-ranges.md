# Optimal Biomarker Ranges for Longevity

These ranges represent longevity-optimized targets, which are often tighter than standard lab reference ranges. Reference ranges indicate disease absence; optimal ranges target maximum healthspan.

## Metabolic Health

| Biomarker | Standard Range | Optimal Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Fasting Glucose | 70-100 mg/dL | 70-90 mg/dL | Lower is better for longevity |
| HbA1c | <5.7% | <5.3% | Reflects 3-month glucose average |
| Fasting Insulin | 2-25 μIU/mL | 2-6 μIU/mL | Lower indicates better insulin sensitivity |
| HOMA-IR | <2.5 | <1.0 | Insulin resistance marker |
| Triglycerides | <150 mg/dL | <70 mg/dL | Lower reduces CVD risk |

## Lipid Panel

| Biomarker | Standard Range | Optimal Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| LDL-C | <100 mg/dL | <70 mg/dL | For high-risk individuals |
| ApoB | <100 mg/dL | <80 mg/dL | Better CVD predictor than LDL-C |
| Lp(a) | <30 mg/dL | <30 mg/dL | Genetic, hard to modify |
| HDL-C | >40 mg/dL (M) | >50 mg/dL | Higher is generally better |
| LDL-P | <1000 nmol/L | <700 nmol/L | Particle number matters |
| Triglyceride/HDL ratio | - | <1.5 | Indicates particle size |

## Inflammation

| Biomarker | Standard Range | Optimal Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| hs-CRP | <3.0 mg/L | <0.5 mg/L | Cardiovascular inflammation |
| Homocysteine | 5-15 μmol/L | <8 μmol/L | Higher linked to CVD, dementia |
| Ferritin | 20-300 ng/mL (M) | 40-100 ng/mL | Too high indicates inflammation |
| ESR | 0-22 mm/hr | <10 mm/hr | General inflammation |
| Fibrinogen | 200-400 mg/dL | 200-300 mg/dL | Clotting and inflammation |

## Hormones

| Biomarker | Standard Range | Optimal Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Free Testosterone (M) | 9-30 ng/dL | 15-25 ng/dL | Age-dependent |
| Total Testosterone (M) | 300-1000 ng/dL | 500-800 ng/dL | Check free T and SHBG too |
| DHEA-S | Varies by age | Upper 50% for age | Declines with age |
| TSH | 0.4-4.0 mIU/L | 1.0-2.0 mIU/L | Optimal thyroid function |
| Free T3 | 2.3-4.2 pg/mL | 3.0-4.0 pg/mL | Active thyroid hormone |
| Free T4 | 0.8-1.8 ng/dL | 1.2-1.5 ng/dL | Thyroid function |
| Cortisol (AM) | 6-23 μg/dL | 10-18 μg/dL | Stress hormone |

| Biomarker | Standard Range | Optimal Range | Notes |

| Vitamin B12 | 200-900 pg/mL | 500-900 pg/mL | Higher end better |
| Folate | >3 ng/mL | 10-20 ng/mL | Works with B12 |
| RBC Magnesium | 4.2-6.8 mg/dL | 5.5-6.5 mg/dL | Better than serum |
| Zinc | 60-130 μg/dL | 90-120 μg/dL | Immune function |
| Iron | 60-170 μg/dL | 80-120 μg/dL | Balance is key |
| Omega-3 Index | - | 8-12% | EPA + DHA in red cells |

## Blood Cells

| RDW | 11-15% | <13% | Red cell variation |
| WBC | 4-11 K/μL | 4-7 K/μL | Lower is generally better |

---

## Important Notes

1. **Individual Variation**: Optimal ranges may vary based on genetics, medications, conditions
2. **Trends Matter**: Look at changes over time, not just single values
3. **Context is Key**: Interpret results in context of symptoms and health history
4. **Consult Professionals**: Use this as a guide, not a replacement for medical advice
5. **Update Regularly**: Longevity science evolves; ranges may change with new research
