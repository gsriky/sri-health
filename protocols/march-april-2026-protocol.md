## 1. NUTRITION PLAN

### Philosophy
Combines Attia's three-lever framework (caloric restriction, time restriction, dietary restriction) with Blueprint's plant-forward approach and Huberman's metabolic optimization principles. Primary goal: reverse insulin resistance and reduce atherogenic lipoproteins.

### Daily Targets
| Macro | Target | Rationale |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| Calories | ~2,200-2,400 kcal/day | Slight deficit if overweight; maintenance if lean |
| Protein | 150-170g (2.0-2.2 g/kg) | Attia target for muscle preservation + metabolic health |
| Carbohydrates | 100-150g (low-glycemic only) | Reduce glucose/insulin load; prediabetic protocol |
| Fat | 70-90g (emphasis on MUFA/PUFA) | Limit saturated fat to <15g/day to lower ApoB |
| Fiber | 40-50g | Soluble fiber binds bile acids, lowers LDL; supports microbiome |

### Eating Window
- **Time-restricted eating**: 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM (9-hour window)
- **Rationale**: Attia's TR lever + Huberman's circadian alignment (eating during active phase)
- **No food after 7:00 PM** — empty stomach improves sleep quality (Attia)

### Daily Meal Structure

**Meal 1 — 10:00 AM (Post-exercise)**
- 3 whole eggs + egg whites (40g protein)
- 1 cup mixed berries (low glycemic, polyphenols)
- 1/2 avocado (MUFA, fiber)
- Handful of raw walnuts (omega-3, ALA)
- Black coffee or green tea (delay caffeine to 90-120 min after waking per Huberman)

**Meal 2 — 1:30 PM**
- 6-8 oz wild-caught salmon or sardines (EPA/DHA, protein) — 3x/week minimum
- OR grilled chicken breast / turkey — remaining days
- Large mixed green salad: spinach, arugula, kale, broccoli sprouts
- 2 tbsp high-polyphenol extra virgin olive oil (Blueprint "Snake Oil" principle)
- 1/2 cup legumes (lentils, black beans) — soluble fiber + plant protein
- Fermented food: sauerkraut, kimchi, or plain kefir (Huberman: 2-4 servings/day for microbiome)

**Meal 3 — 6:30 PM (Light)**
- 6 oz lean protein (chicken, fish, tofu)
- Roasted cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts)
- 1/2 cup quinoa or sweet potato (moderate complex carb)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Finish eating by 7:00 PM

### Specific Dietary Rules

**PRIORITIZE (daily):**
- High-polyphenol EVOO: 2-3 tbsp/day (Blueprint Tier 1 — most impactful single food)
- Fatty fish: 3-4 servings/week (EPA/DHA for triglycerides, inflammation, HDL)
- Soluble fiber: oats, legumes, flaxseed, psyllium (10-15g soluble specifically)
- Fermented foods: 2-4 servings/day (Huberman — microbiome diversity)
- Cruciferous vegetables: daily (liver support, sulforaphane)
- Berries: daily (anthocyanins, low glycemic)
- Nuts: 1 oz/day walnuts or almonds (cardiovascular benefit)

**ELIMINATE:**
- Refined sugar and sweetened beverages (glucose/insulin management)
- Refined grains (white bread, pasta, white rice)
- Processed foods (trans fats, seed oils in processed form)
- Alcohol — ZERO for this protocol period (Attia: "potent carcinogen, devastates sleep"; liver markers elevated)
- Excess saturated fat: limit red meat to 1x/week max, no butter, limit cheese

**LIMIT:**
- Fruit juice (concentrated sugar)
- Dried fruit (high glycemic load)
- Total fructose intake (uric acid at 6.5 — fructose drives uric acid synthesis)

### Specific Foods for Sri's Biomarkers

| Biomarker Target | Food Strategy |
|------------------|---------------|
| Lower ApoB/LDL-P | Reduce saturated fat <15g/day; increase soluble fiber; plant sterols (2g/day) |
| Lower triglycerides | Fatty fish, reduce refined carbs, eliminate alcohol |
| Improve HDL/HDL-P | EVOO, fatty fish, nuts, aerobic exercise |
| Lower glucose/HbA1c | Low-GI carbs only, fiber with every meal, no refined carbs |
| Lower uric acid | Reduce fructose, limit purines (organ meats, shellfish), hydrate well |

## 2. SLEEP SCHEDULE

### Philosophy
Attia: "Sleep is the foundation everything else builds upon." Huberman: Behavioral interventions first, supplements second. Blueprint: 8+ hours, consistent timing.

### Schedule
| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 6:00 AM | Wake (consistent, even weekends) |
| 6:00-6:30 AM | Morning sunlight viewing — 10-15 min outdoor light (Huberman: #1 circadian tool) |
| 9:00 PM | Begin wind-down: dim all lights, blue-light blocking glasses |
| 9:30 PM | No screens. Reading, stretching, or journaling only |
| 10:00 PM | In bed, lights out |
| **Target**: 8 hours sleep (10 PM - 6 AM) | |

### Sleep Environment (Attia + Huberman)
- **Temperature**: 65-67°F / 18-19°C (cool room critical for sleep onset)
- **Darkness**: Complete — blackout curtains + sleep mask
- **Sound**: White noise machine or silence
- **Mattress cooling**: Consider OOLER/Eight Sleep pad (Attia uses)
- **No phone in bedroom** (charge in another room)

### Evening Routine
1. **7:00 PM** — Last meal (empty stomach improves sleep per Attia)
2. **8:00 PM** — Optional: Sauna session 15-20 min if available (Attia: temperature drop drives sleep onset; 4-6x/week for mortality benefit)
3. **9:00 PM** — Dim lights to <10 lux, blue-light blocking glasses on
4. **9:15 PM** — Magnesium L-threonate (see supplements section)
5. **9:30 PM** — 10-min NSDR/Yoga Nida session (Huberman: increases dopamine 65%, reduces anxiety, accelerates sleep)
6. **10:00 PM** — Lights out

### Morning Protocol (Huberman Circadian Reset)
1. **6:00 AM** — Wake at same time daily (even weekends — circadian consistency)
2. **6:05 AM** — Outdoor light exposure: 10-15 min (cloudy: 20-30 min). Do NOT wear sunglasses. Walk or stand outside
3. **6:15 AM** — Hydrate: 16-24 oz water with pinch of sea salt and squeeze of lemon
4. **7:30-8:00 AM** — First caffeine (90-120 min delay after waking per Huberman — prevents afternoon crash, allows natural cortisol awakening response)

### Critical Sleep Rules
- **No caffeine after 2:00 PM** (half-life 5-6 hours)
- **No alcohol** (even 1 drink reduces deep sleep and REM by 20-40%)
- **No bright/overhead lights 11 PM - 4 AM** (Huberman: critical dark period)
- **If you wake at night**: NSDR protocol (10 min) rather than phone/screens
- **Consistent 7 days/week** — no "catching up" on weekends

## 7. RISKS & CONTRAINDICATIONS

### Supplement Interactions to Monitor
- **Berberine + any future statin**: Both are hepatically metabolized. Monitor liver enzymes (ALT, AST). Berberine inhibits CYP3A4 — can increase statin levels. If statin prescribed, discuss berberine dose adjustment with physician
- **Berberine + metformin**: Additive glucose-lowering — risk of hypoglycemia. Don't combine without physician guidance
- **High-dose omega-3 (>3g)**: Mild increased bleeding risk. Inform physician/dentist before any procedures
- **Magnesium (total ~544mg elemental/day)**: Can cause loose stools. Split dosing mitigates this
- **L-citrulline + blood pressure meds**: Additive hypotensive effect if on antihypertensives

### Warning Signs — Contact Physician If:
- Fasting glucose >115 mg/dL or symptoms of hyperglycemia
- Muscle pain/weakness (if statin started — rhabdomyolysis screening)
- Persistent GI distress from berberine >2 weeks
- Heart palpitations, chest pain, or unusual shortness of breath during exercise
- Signs of hypoglycemia (shakiness, sweating, confusion) — especially if berberine + exercise
- Yellowing skin/eyes (hepatic concern)

### Exercise Precautions
- **Do NOT jump to Week 7-8 intensity** — follow the 8-week progression
- No maximal lifting until movement patterns are solid (Weeks 1-4 bodyweight/light)
- If dizzy or lightheaded during VO2max intervals, stop and return to Zone 2
- Consider cardiac stress test before VO2max intervals given cardiovascular risk profile (discuss with physician)
